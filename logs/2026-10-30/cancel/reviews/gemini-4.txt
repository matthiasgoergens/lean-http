- **Direct `Waiter` registration and cleanup ([`Cancel.watch`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Cancel.lean#L109-L125), [`Cancel.finish`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Cancel.lean#L139-L151))**: In [`Std.Sync.CancellationToken`](file:///home/matthias/.elan/toolchains/leanprover--lean4---v4.34.1/src/lean/Std/Sync/CancellationToken.lean#L164-L191), both `selector.registerFn` and `selector.unregisterFn` consist solely of `BaseIO` operations inside `token.state.atomically`. Under `EAsync.toBaseIO` ([`start`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Wait.lean#L30-L31)), `BaseAsync.bind` and `EAsync.tryFinally'` reduce `MaybeTask.pure` inline without spawning tasks, so registration in `watch` and unregistration in `finish` execute synchronously on the calling thread. At call end, `waiter.race` marks `waiter.finished := true` and resolves `waiter.promise` (releasing the `chainTask` closure on `promise.result?`), and `unregisterFn` removes the finished waiter from `token.state.consumers`. A call without a caller token allocates only three `IO.Ref`s and spawns nothing.
- **No deadlock with `CancellationToken.cancel` ([`Cancel.fire`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Cancel.lean#L91-L101))**: [`CancellationToken.cancel`](file:///home/matthias/.elan/toolchains/leanprover--lean4---v4.34.1/src/lean/Std/Sync/CancellationToken.lean#L108-L126) resolves each consumer inside `x.state.atomically` while holding the non-recursive `Std.Mutex` and *before* `set st` writes `st.reason := some reason`. Neither `fire`, the bell's continuation in [`Cancel.race`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Cancel.lean#L214-L216) (`winner.resolve (.cancelled .cancel)`), nor the mailbox hook in [`Live.begin`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Body.lean#L389-L391) touches `token.state`. #### 2. [Low / Documentation] `Cancel.state` and `Cancel.reason?` docstrings slightly overstate when `state` caches the reason
- **File & lines**: [`Cancel.state`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Cancel.lean#L59-L61) and [`Cancel.reason?`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Cancel.lean#L153-L155)

  - When a call is fired by a watched token ([`Cancel.watch`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-notes/cancel/review4/tree/LeanHttp/Cancel.lean#L121)), `fire` is called with `reason := none`. This has no effect on runtime behaviour (since `finish` runs in `finally` as the call returns), but the docstrings on `state` and `reason?` should note that `state` only records `some (some reason)` when fired with a reason via `Cancel.cancel`.
