  - **Non-idempotent requests and at-most-once redispatch:** [`redispatch?`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-wt-pool/LeanHttp/Client.lean#L512-L520) only allows `.closedBeforeResponse` when `!received && f.delivery == .maybeDelivered && (f.cause matches .peerClosed) && method.isIdempotent`, and allows `.requestTimeout` only on a 408 with `interim == 0`. `again` requires `held.reused && !progress.redispatched`, and the recursive call passes `reuse := false` and `redispatched := true` (lines 756, 768), so redispatch cannot happen more than once.
  - **Mutex and active-cap wake-ups:** None of the `client.withState` critical sections (`stats`, `close`, `giveBack`, `dropConn`, `takeConn`) block or await inside the mutex. `takeConn` reads `s.freed` while holding the mutex, and `giveBack`, `dropConn`, and `close` all call [`wake`](file:///home/matthias/prog/lean-http-wt-pool/LeanHttp/Client.lean#L199-L201) (resolving the current `freed` promise and installing a fresh one) while holding the mutex, so a call that observes `.atCap` cannot miss a subsequent wake-up.
