    1. `.closedBeforeResponse`: the first attempt had `f.delivery == .maybeDelivered`, meaning bytes of the request were written to the first socket and no response byte arrived. If the second attempt fails with `e.delivery == .notSent` (e.g., deadline passed in `acquire` or `connect`, client closed, active-cap wait timed out, DNS or connect failed, or `Exchange.begin` expired at the deadline), upgrading `e.delivery` from `.notSent` to `.maybeDelivered` is exact because the first attempt may have been delivered to the server.     3. `.notSent`: the first attempt had `f.delivery == .notSent` (nothing written on the first attempt). If the second attempt also has `e.delivery == .notSent`, neither attempt wrote a byte, so keeping `.notSent` is accurate.
