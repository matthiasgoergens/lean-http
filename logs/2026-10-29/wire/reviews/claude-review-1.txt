Review by a fresh Claude (general-purpose subagent, fresh context), 2026-10-29, of the working tree
snapshotted at /tmp/lean-http-wire-review (the brief is prompt-1.txt).  Line numbers refer to that
snapshot.  Its report, condensed:

1. After a 408 re-dispatch, the call's error can say notSent although a request reached the server
   (guarantee 6).  Client.lean Attempt.run: `again` raises delivery to maybeDelivered only for
   closedBeforeResponse, and progress.furthest is updated from the final exchange alone.  A POST
   answered by a stale 408 on a reused connection, re-dispatched, whose fresh connect is refused,
   fails with delivery notSent.  High.  Fix: raise furthest by the first exchange before `again`.
2. A receive can outlive withResponse when another task is reading as the callback ends
   (guarantee 4).  Body.lean release/finishRead: release sees `busy` and does nothing; the other
   task's recv? stays registered until its read completes, the read gap or the deadline, and the
   connection stays checked out.  The module doc overclaims.  High.  Fix: cancel the receive from
   release, or have release wait for the read.
3. Call.validate cannot see re-dispatches, so a re-dispatch bug passes the trace check: only one
   send is logged per planner exchange.  High.  Fix: a redispatch event, checked against
   redispatch? from the recorded first outcome, the method and reuse.
4. The trace's head check compares the planner's head with itself: the shell logs hop.head, not
   what went on the wire (Client.plan, then Exchange.prepare).  High that it is vacuous, medium that
   it matters.  Trace.lean's module doc and the new sentence in Call/Proofs.lean overclaim.  Fix:
   log what was encoded and recompute it in validate.
5. validate does not check the error's delivery (nor hops, retries), and accepts any invalidRequest
   refusal after a redirect.  High (medium-low for the refusal).
6. validate has no upper bound on retry waits.  Medium.
7. The catch blocks of Live.abandon and BodyStream.advance cancel through a stale `live` whose
   `reading` may be none while a newer receive is pending.  Medium that it is wrong, low that it is
   reachable.  Fix: cancel through the socket unconditionally.
8. A connection can leak if reading the wall clock throws after the response head: neither
   discardBody nor BodyStream runs.  Medium if it throws, low that it does.
9. An unreachable branch in Attempt.run (.response, no live) would leak the held connection.
10. The escaped stream keeps a dropped connection's socket open: BodyStream.settle closes over
    `held`, so a stream kept after the callback keeps the descriptor.  Medium.
11. Live.next adds a continuation to long-pending tasks on every wait (awaitFirst chains one per
    call): a write stalled after an early response gains one per read of a long download.  Medium.
12. The stream can hold more than one read: pieces of one read pending, and the leftover credit's
    next read completed in `reading`.  Medium-low.
13. Redispatch.notSent cannot arise with the current exchange core.  Low impact.
14. Each planner retry of a connect failure runs a fresh DNS lookup; Connected.rounds and the rounds
    of ConnectError are now dead.  Medium (a behaviour change).
15. Minor: Progress.attempts grows across waits (small, bounded); the test's withTraces copies per
    event; request's catch-all error has seed 0 and no target; the trace ends at .deliver when the
    call then ends with a stream error.  Low.
16. Misleading docstrings: Body.lean says Client.request asks the planner (it is CallSpec.loop);
    Config.deadlineMs is a long line and names only Client.request; Error.notRetried's list of none
    cases is incomplete; run.sh attributes the 307 body check to redirect-303; Call/Proofs.lean (see
    4).
17. Missing tests: the concurrent-read race, the 408 re-dispatch error's delivery, a retried 503 or
    3xx whose body exceeds drainBytes, reading past the deadline in withResponse, validate controls
    for a wrong delivery, hops or retries.

Guarantees it checked and found to hold apart from these: the requests are built from st.current
with the hop's body, the decision clock is read after the exchange, a retry sleeps until
min (clock.now + delay) deadline; discardBody runs before every follow, retry and fail; every
connection is given back or dropped once on the paths traced (but 8 and 9); no receive is pending
in a single-task use (Live.begin returns at the head with no read in flight, perform cancels on
finish and fail, abandon and idleClean cancel); each read gives credit, release runs after the
callback normally or by an exception, a released stream refuses use; no unsafe resend (the planner,
and re-dispatch only for idempotent methods or after a 408); body data does not grow across waits;
every wait is bounded by nextDeadline.
