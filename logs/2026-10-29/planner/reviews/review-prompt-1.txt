You are reviewing the theorem statements of a pure "per-call planner" for an HTTP/1.1 client written in Lean 4 (v4.34.1). Read only; do not modify anything.

Files: LeanHttp/Call.lean (the planner: State, Outcome, Step, start, plan, Reachable), LeanHttp/Call/Redirect.lean (redirect decision, header steps), LeanHttp/Call/RetryAfter.lean, LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean (the theorems), LeanHttp/Backoff.lean, LeanHttp/Error.lean and LeanHttp/Exchange.lean (Failure, Cause, Delivery). All proofs compile with no sorry and only propext, Classical.choice and Quot.sound; you do not need to check the proofs, only the statements.

The rules the theorems are meant to establish:
1. Credential fields (Authorization, Cookie, Proxy-Authorization) never appear on a request to a different origin than the one they were given for (the origin of the call's first request); https to http counts as a different origin.
2. The hop count (redirects followed) never exceeds the limit, and reaching it fails with an error rather than delivering the last 3xx.
3. A request whose method is not idempotent, or whose body is not replayable, is never retried after any byte may have been written.
4. A failure whose cause is localClose is never retried.
5. Every planned delay is within [0, remaining time before the deadline] and within the backoff cap (or the Retry-After limit for Retry-After delays).
6. A followed 303 always yields GET, or HEAD for HEAD.
7. A method-changing redirect carries no body and no content headers.
Plus: retries never cross a redirect; Host follows the origin; the retry counters stay within limits.
