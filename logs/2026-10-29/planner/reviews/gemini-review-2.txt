### 1. Assessment of First-Round Conclusions

I agree with the **Refuted**, **Out of scope**, and **Kept by design** conclusions from Round 1:
- **3xx at the hop limit that would not be followed**: Delivering a 304 or an unfollowable 3xx at `hops = maxRedirects` is right; [`plan_hop_limit`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L620-L629) properly scopes the failure to redirects where `decideRedirect ... = .follow next`.
- **`Host` absence & [`Reachable.target`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L851-L852)**: [`plan_follow_host`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L749-L753) + [`Reachable.target`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L851-L852) accurately reflect the split between [`Call`](file://LeanHttp/Call.lean) and [`Client.plan`](file://LeanHttp/Client.lean#L110-L127).
- **`Retry-After` relative to `Date`** and **307/308 POST redirects**: Both match RFC 9110 and are clearly documented in [`Proofs.lean`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L40-L42).

**Where I disagree with the Round 1 "Fixed" conclusions:**
1. **"Rule 7 held only on the hop where the method changed: added `Reachable.emptyBody` for every later hop"** (and [DESIGN.md:48](file://DESIGN.md#L48): *"Proved (`LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean`): ... a method change leaves no body and no content field, on that hop and the ones after it"*):
   [`Reachable.emptyBody`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L879-L880) takes `(he : st.current.body = .empty)` as a **hypothesis** and proves only `∀ e ∈ st.current.head.headers.toList, e.1 ∉ contentNames`. No theorem states that once `st.current.body = .empty`, subsequent steps preserve `body = .empty` (i.e. `st.current.body = .empty → (plan st clock outcome).1.current.body = .empty`). On a later hop where the method does not change again (e.g., `POST → 303 → GET → 302 → GET`), [`plan_follow_method_change`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L729-L733) does not apply, and nothing in [`Proofs.lean`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean) proves that the second `GET` still has `body = .empty` (or therefore satisfies `Reachable.emptyBody`'s hypothesis).
2. **[`plan_follow_replayable`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L814-L820) docstring vs. statement**:
   Its third sentence claims *"A redirect that keeps the method of a request with a streamed body is delivered instead"*, which is stronger than its statement (`plan ... = (st', .follow next) → next.replayable st.bodyKind = true`).

### 3. Findings Ordered by Importance

*(Note: test files `call-tests` and `call-diff` are not in this directory snapshot, so test coverage cannot be verified directly.)*

1. **Missing forward preservation of `body = .empty` for Rule 7 on later hops** ([Proofs.lean:874-911](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L874-L911), [DESIGN.md:48](file://DESIGN.md#L48)) — *Confidence: High*
   [DESIGN.md:48](file://DESIGN.md#L48) claims it is **Proved** that *"a method change leaves no body and no content field, on that hop and the ones after it"*. [`plan_follow_method_change`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L729-L733) proves `next.body = .empty` on the hop where the method changes, and [`Reachable.emptyBody`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L879-L880) proves `st.current.body = .empty → ∀ e ∈ ..., e.1 ∉ contentNames`. Missing is the bridge theorem `st.current.body = .empty → (plan st clock outcome).1.current.body = .empty` showing `body = .empty` is invariant once entered.

2. **Hop-by-hop credential headers nominated in `Connection` are restored on `Origin A → Origin B → Origin A`** ([Call.lean:325](file://LeanHttp/Call.lean#L325), [Redirect.lean:30-31](file://LeanHttp/Call/Redirect.lean#L30-L31), [Redirect.lean:304-306](file://LeanHttp/Call/Redirect.lean#L304-L306), [DESIGN.md:46](file://DESIGN.md#L46)) — *Confidence: High*
   [Redirect.lean:30-31](file://LeanHttp/Call/Redirect.lean#L30-L31) and [DESIGN.md:46](file://DESIGN.md#L46) state that *"Hop-by-hop fields (`Connection`, `Keep-Alive`, `Transfer-Encoding` and those `Connection` names) never carry over."* However, [`Call.start`](file://LeanHttp/Call.lean#L325) records `given := credentials head.headers.toList` before stripping `nominatedNames`. If the initial request has `Connection: authorization` (or `cookie`), a same-origin redirect drops `Authorization`, but a cross-origin redirect followed by a redirect back to `originalOrigin` (`toOriginal = true`, `fromOriginal = false`) appends `given` in [`credentialStep`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Redirect.lean#L304-L306), resurrecting the nominated hop-by-hop field.

3. **Unproved docstring sentence in [`plan_follow_replayable`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L814-L820)** — *Confidence: High*
   The docstring states *"A redirect that keeps the method of a request with a streamed body is delivered instead"*, whereas the theorem only proves `plan st clock outcome = (st', .follow next) → next.replayable st.bodyKind = true`.

4. **Other non-retry conditions in [DESIGN.md:47](file://DESIGN.md#L47) and [Call.lean:26-28](file://LeanHttp/Call.lean#L26-L28) have no top-level `plan_*` theorem** ([Proofs.lean:391-396](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L391-L396)) — *Confidence: High*
   [DESIGN.md:47](file://DESIGN.md#L47) states that *"a response head received, a `localClose`, the call's deadline, a name that did not resolve, an invalid request, a malformed response and a limit, never"* are retried. Only `localClose` has a `plan_*` theorem ([`plan_localClose`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L655-L656)); `f.delivery = .responseReceived` and non-retryable causes appear only inside the helper [`retryFailure_ok`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L391-L396). Similarly, [`mem_crossOriginStep`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L97-L98) (`Referer` and cache validators dropped cross-origin) is proved as a helper for [`mem_nextFields`](file://LeanHttp/Call/Proofs.lean#L145-L151) but never exposed as a `plan_follow_*` theorem.
