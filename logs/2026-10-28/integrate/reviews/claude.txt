**Agree (high confidence)**

- **`step_finish_reusable` and `Reusable`** (Proofs.lean:1273, :123).  The fields correspond one to one to the conjuncts of `reusableNow` (Exchange.lean:518): keep-alive configured, `parser.reusable`, `held.isEmpty`, `written == size`, `!early`, `!inputEnded`.  `head.isSome` is an extra field.  So `Reusable` is exactly as strong as the reuse decision, plus the head.
- **`observed_finish_reusable`** (Proofs.lean:2210).  The statement says what the docstring says.
- **`Obs.fed` and `Obs.body`.**  `Obs.observe` (Proofs.lean ~1815) appends a `received b` exactly when `Model.observe` does (Check.lean:106-111): not when `b` is empty, not after end of input, and not once the run has ended.  This matches `Exchange.step`: stale or empty reads are ignored, terminal states ignore everything, and bytes after `inputEnded` are skipped (Exchange.lean:717, 727, 883).  Bytes received during `.sending` are counted but fail the exchange, which only touches clauses that apply while the exchange has not failed.  `fedAfter` and the `hfed` step in `Observed.cinv` check this alignment.  `Obs.body` is every `deliverBody` payload in order.
- **`Observed.cinv`** (Response.lean:710).  The `CInv` fields match the docstring.  `parser` and `body` are stated with `ex.inputEnded`, which `Agrees.eofSeen` equates with the model's end of input.
- **`observed_finish_complete`** (Response.lean:844).  The statement matches the prose, including "none after a body delimited by the end of input" (`hd.framing = .untilClose → extra = []`).
- **`observed_body_prefix`** (Response.lean:894).  The statement matches; it is not trivial, because `rest` is forced to be the held bytes by `CInv.body`.

**Findings**

1. The framing and keep-alive in `observed_finish_codec` are not tied to the head bytes (Response.lean:793-798; module docstring lines 19-21).  Medium confidence that this overclaims; high confidence that the statement lacks the link.  `HeadBytesOf hd.http10 hd.code hd.reason hd.fields hb` ties only those four parts of `hd` to the bytes; `hd.framing`, `hd.keepAlive` and `hd.response` are free in the statement.  The link exists as `HeadOK` (Codec/Proofs/Body.lean:63), inside the codec invariant `Inv`; it can be had from `(parsed_reach …).good.inv.1 hd hhd`, but it is not in the conclusion.  Fix: add `Codec.HeadOK` to the conclusion.  `observed_finish_complete` has the same gap.
2. "Every head allowed keep-alive" is not proved for interim heads (Proofs.lean:127-129, :1265).  Medium confidence.  `Parser.reusable` checks only `head.any (·.keepAlive)`; the code conjoins the interim heads' keep-alive (Codec/Head.lean:64) but no theorem says so.
3. "The head the core delivered" is the core's `head` field (Response.lean:797, :853).  Low confidence that it matters; true by `onHead`, and `step_deliverHead` presumably carries the link.
4. The `observed_finish_complete` docstring claims the checker's "finish only after a complete response" without stating the end-of-input part (`needsEnd → eofSeen`).  Low confidence; implied through the codec's `run_noUCDone`, but no conjunct says it.
5. `observed_body_prefix` points at `Codec.feed_respSound`; when `m.eofSeen` is true, `parsed` includes `finish`'s events, for which `finish_respSound` is the matching lemma.  Low confidence.
6. `Obs.fed`'s docstring says "every byte received before the end of input", but bytes received after the run ended are not counted either.  Low confidence; `Model.fed`'s docstring omits both conditions.

**Reuse decision versus restated `Reusable`: as strong (high confidence).**  `Reusable` mirrors `reusableNow` one for one.  In `observed_finish_codec`, `response_sound` together with `extra = false` gives `fed = pre ++ hb ++ body` with nothing after it, and the body delivered is the payload unless abandoned.  An abandoned reusable finish (after draining) is possible, and the docstring correctly excludes it from the body clause.
