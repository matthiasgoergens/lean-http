### Summary

All six theorem statements agree with their docstrings and with the module docstring of [Response.lean](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange/Response.lean#L7-L34) (up to two minor prose nuances noted below). ### Specific Checks

- **Vacuity:** None. [`Observed.begin`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange/Proofs.lean#L1832-L1834) and [`Observed.step`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange/Proofs.lean#L1836-L1838) are total over all inputs and event traces; all hypotheses (`Inv ex`, `.finish _ ∈ ...`, `Live ex`, `m.abandoned = false`) are reachable in normal execution.
- **`Obs.fed` and `Obs.body`:**
  - [`Obs.observe`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange/Proofs.lean#L1808-L1819) updates `fed`, `eofSeen`, `ended`, and `abandoned` identically to [`Model.observe`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/Tests/Support/Check.lean#L98-L115): it appends `b` on `.received b` iff `!m.ended && !b.isEmpty && !m.eofSeen` (an empty `b` has no bytes, and [`Exchange.step`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange.lean#L877-L889) / [`handle`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange.lean#L714-L727) ignores events after `terminal` and ignores `.received` after `inputEnded` or when `b.isEmpty`). Note that if unsolicited bytes arrive in `.sending`, `Obs.observe` appends them to `fed` while `handle` fails without parsing them; [`CInv`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange/Response.lean#L169-L183) accounts for this via its `Live ex` guard.
  - [`Obs.see`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange/Proofs.lean#L1822-L1827) appends `b.data.toList` on `.deliverBody b` and nothing else ([`see_body`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange/Response.lean#L695-L701)), matching [`checkStep`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/Tests/Support/Check.lean#L197).
- **Codec Predicates in `observed_finish_codec`:**
  - `m'.fed.data.toList = pre ++ hb ++ body` has no trailing `extra` list (proved `extra = []` from `r.1.reusable = true → r.1.extra = false` and [`Codec.response_sound`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Codec/Proofs/ResponseSound.lean#L736-L747)).
  - [`Codec.InterimHeads`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Codec/Proofs/HeadSound.lean#L646-L653) `pre hd.interim` means `pre` is `hd.interim` valid 1xx (non-101) heads.
  - [`Codec.HeadBytesOf`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Codec/Proofs/HeadSound.lean#L637-L640) means `hb` is a valid HTTP/1.0 or 1.1 status line (`hd.code`, `hd.reason`), valid field lines for `hd.fields`, and CRLF; via [`Reach.good`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Codec/Proofs/Body.lean#L451-L456) and [`HeadOK`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Codec/Proofs/Body.lean#L63-L71), `hd.framing` and `hd.response` are the framing and `Std.Http.Response.Head` of those fields.
  - [`Codec.BodyOf`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Codec/Proofs/ResponseSound.lean#L424-L429) `hd.framing body m'.body` means `body` is empty with `m'.body = []` (`.none`), `n` bytes equal to `m'.body` (`.length n`), or a complete chunked body with chunk-size lines, chunk data concatenating to `m'.body`, 0-chunk line, and trailer section (`.chunked`).
- **`Reusable` vs. `Exchange.reusableNow`:** [`Reusable`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange/Proofs.lean#L123-L140) contains all six conditions of [`Exchange.reusableNow`](file:///tmp/integ/review-snap/LeanHttp/Exchange.lean#L518-L520) plus `head : ex.head.isSome = true`.
